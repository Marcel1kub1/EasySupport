- Inter body font: existing local assets in `assets/fonts/`.

## Visual system

- Accent: restrained violet sampled from the supplied EasySupport logo; it is reserved for focus, active states, links, and selected controls.
- Surfaces: near-charcoal page, elevated graphite panels, and quiet neutral borders.
- Typography: Cabinet Grotesk for display and short headings; locally hosted Inter for interface copy and controls.
- Shape: compact 7px control corners, 12px grouped surfaces, and 18px image frames.
- Motion: short button and focus transitions; GSAP scroll reveals and a pinned, stacked product-image moment on the public landing page. All movement is disabled for `prefers-reduced-motion`.

The supplied product screenshots remain the source of product imagery. Dashboard panels use actual guild data; no community counts, testimonials, or product screenshots are fabricated.
